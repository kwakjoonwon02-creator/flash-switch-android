Privacy Policy for Flash Switch

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Flash Switch is a flashlight utility app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles permissions, advertising, and data.

Camera permission

Flash Switch requests the Android Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) only to control the device flashlight/torch. The app does not take photos, record videos, save camera images, or upload camera content.

Advertising

Flash Switch uses Google AdMob to show banner ads. Google AdMob may collect or use device and advertising-related data to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to Google's policies.

You can learn more about Google's data practices here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising technologies

Data collected by this app

Flash Switch itself does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, photos, videos, precise location, contacts, or files.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data as described in their own privacy policies.

Children

Flash Switch is not specifically directed to children. If you configure the app for child-directed audiences in Google Play Console, make sure your ads and Play Console declarations comply with Google Play Families and ads policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, permissions, or third-party services change. Updates will be posted on this page.
